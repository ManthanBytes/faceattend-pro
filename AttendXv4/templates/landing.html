Attendance tools for students and teachers

Bring common classroom attendance tasks into one place. Available features depend on the account role and the institution's configuration.

🧑‍💻

Face recognition

Use the face-matching attendance flow to help identify enrolled students during a class session.

▦

QR verification

Provide a QR-based option for active sessions, with configurable QR token lifetime.

📡

Live class sessions

Teachers can start and manage sessions for their assigned class and subject.

📊

Attendance reports

Review attendance records and session summaries to understand student participation.

📅

Digital timetables

Keep class schedules, subjects, periods, and room details organised in the app.

🏫

Role-based screens

Separate workflows are provided for administrators, teachers, and students.

How FaceAttend works

A straightforward workflow for everyday classroom attendance.

1. Set up accounts

An administrator manages institution settings and reviews teacher registrations.

2. Start a class session

A teacher starts a session for the relevant class and subject.

3. Record and review

Attendance can be marked through the supported face or QR flow, then reviewed in records and reports.
